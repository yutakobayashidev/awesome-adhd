# The effect of intelligent monitoring of physical exercise on executive function in children with ADHD

- Source: arXiv
- arXiv ID: 2503.09079v1
- Authors: Liwen Lin; Nan Lib; Shuchen Zhao
- Published: 2025-03-12
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.09079v1

## Abstract

Children with ADHD often struggle with executive function and motor skills, impacting academics and social life. The paper examines short- and long-term effects of three physical activity interventions: a specific skill training group, a low-demand exercise group, and a control group over 12 weeks. The specific skill training group reportedly showed significant improvements in motor tasks and working memory, while the other groups showed smaller changes. Long-term physical activity improved working memory, while short-term physical activity had limited effects on balance and manual dexterity. Smart devices tracked progress, confirming sustained engagement and improvement in the specific skill training group.

## Curation note

This source is child-focused and appears as a preprint. It is useful as raw background on exercise and monitoring, but the wiki already has a stronger adult ADHD exercise systematic review/meta-analysis; this source should not be treated as adult evidence or as treatment advice.
