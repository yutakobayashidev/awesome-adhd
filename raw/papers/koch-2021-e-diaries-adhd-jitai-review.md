## Abstract

ADHD symptoms are dynamic in nature: states of hyperactivity, inattention and impulsivity as core symptoms, and emotion dysregulation as an associated feature. Although much work has investigated between-subject differences, less is known about relationships between symptoms, triggers and contexts. Understanding temporal associations between symptoms and environmental triggers in ecologically valid ways may be the basis for just-in-time adaptive interventions. The review describes ambulatory assessment methodology, summarizes ADHD literature, and discusses mobile sensing, real-time analyses and just-in-time adaptive interventions.
