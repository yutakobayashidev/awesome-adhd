# Guiding Empowerment Model: Liberating Neurodiversity in Online Higher Education

- Subjects: cs.AI; cs.HC

## Abstract

This paper addresses equity gaps for neurodivergent and situationally limited learners in online higher education. It argues that one-size-fits-all educational approaches leave disabled students to self-advocate or tolerate inadequate support, and that existing frameworks do not sufficiently address holistic environmental needs or technology interventions for disclosed and undisclosed learning or developmental disabilities.

Using secondary research of around 100 articles, the authors introduce a Guiding Empowerment Model with cognitive and situational factors that contextualize day-to-day learner ability. They synthesize sample student profiles and use the model to evaluate learning-platform features and supportive technology solutions. The approach augments Universal Design for Learning with factors such as sensory processing differences, social connection challenges, and environmental limitations. Proposed technology-enabled features include customizable task management, guided varied content access, and guided multi-modal collaboration.
