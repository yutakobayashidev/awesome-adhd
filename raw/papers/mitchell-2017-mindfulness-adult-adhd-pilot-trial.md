## Abstract

This study assessed feasibility, acceptability, and preliminary efficacy of mindfulness meditation for ADHD, executive functioning, and emotion dysregulation symptoms in an adult ADHD sample. Adults with ADHD were stratified by medication status and randomized to an 8-week group-based mindfulness treatment (n = 11) or waitlist group (n = 9). Feasibility and acceptability were positive. Self-reported ADHD and executive function symptoms, clinician ratings, and self-reported emotion dysregulation improved for the treatment group relative to waitlist with large effect sizes. Improvement was not observed for executive function tasks. Findings require larger trials.
