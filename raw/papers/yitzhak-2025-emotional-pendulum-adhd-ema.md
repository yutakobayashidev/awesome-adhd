# The Emotional Pendulum in ADHD: Insights From EMA of Emotional States in Young Adults

- Source type: PubMed metadata/abstract
- PubMed ID: 40849121
- DOI: 10.1016/j.beth.2025.03.004
- Journal: Behavior Therapy
- Published: 2025
- Authors: Neta Yitzhak; Maayan Cohen; Ruth Traub Bar-Ilan; Adina Maeir; Eran Eldar; Mor Nahum

## Abstract

Emotion dysregulation, especially emotional instability, characterizes adults with ADHD. This study used ecological momentary assessment (EMA) to track emotional states and examine emotional instability over time. Young adults with ADHD (n = 57) and without ADHD (n = 54) completed a self-report questionnaire for emotion regulation difficulties followed by a 5-day EMA protocol with five emotion reports per day. Individuals with ADHD showed significantly higher intra-individual emotional variability, but no group differences in emotional lability between and across days. Higher emotional variability was linked to self-reported emotion regulation difficulties in the ADHD group. Cluster analysis found higher probability of ADHD participants being in a cluster characterized by elevated emotional variability and emotion regulation difficulties. The authors state that young adults with ADHD may experience a broader range of emotions in daily life and that understanding these dynamics could support personalized therapeutic strategies and intervention design.

## Curation note

Relevant to emotion regulation, EMA, daily-life variability, and personalized supports. It does not imply that any specific self-help or app intervention is effective.
