## Abstract

This systematic review investigated effectiveness and user experience outcomes of immersive VR interventions for cognitive rehabilitation in people with ADHD. Searches through November 2024 identified 1046 records; 15 articles met inclusion criteria. Immersive VR interventions were generally effective in improving cognitive abilities such as attention, memory, and executive functioning. User experience outcomes were generally positive, with low levels of simulator sickness and minimal attrition reported. More studies are required to examine longitudinal impact beyond treatment cessation.
