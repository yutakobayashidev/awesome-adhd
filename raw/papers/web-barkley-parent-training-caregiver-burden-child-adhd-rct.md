# The effect of the web-based Barkley behavioral training model on the caregiving burden and resilience of mothers of children with ADHD: a randomized controlled trial

- PMID: 42218422
- DOI: 10.1007/s10803-019-03976-4
- Source URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42218422/
- Journal: BMC Psychiatry
- Published: 2026
- Candidate ID: pubmed:42218422

## Abstract

Mothers of children with ADHD often face communication challenges and caregiving burden. This randomized controlled trial tested whether a web-based multimedia training program based on Barkley's behavioral parent training model affected caregiving burden and resilience.

Fifty-six mothers were randomly allocated to intervention and control groups. The intervention group received eight web-based multimedia sessions. Questionnaires were completed at baseline, post-intervention, and one-month follow-up. Four participants from each group withdrew. Immediately after intervention, resilience improved in the intervention group, while caregiving burden did not show a significant between-group difference. One month later, significant between-group differences favored the intervention group for both caregiving burden and resilience. The authors suggest that low-cost, accessible, flexible web-based training may support mothers, while recommending larger studies with longer follow-up.
