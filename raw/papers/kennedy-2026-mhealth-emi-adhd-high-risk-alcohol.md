# From Assessment to Intervention: Leveraging EMA to Develop a Personalized mHealth EMI for Young Adults With ADHD and High-Risk Alcohol Use

- Source type: PubMed metadata/abstract
- PubMed ID: 40880228
- DOI recorded by PubMed: 10.1177/1745691621994226
- Journal: Journal of Studies on Alcohol and Drugs
- Published: 2026
- Authors: Traci M. Kennedy; Christine M. Lee; Brooke S. G. Molina; Sarah L. Pedersen

## Abstract

Ecological momentary assessment (EMA) can collect data on alcohol use and its proximal precursors and consequences. EMA can also inform and deploy mobile-health interventions. This article describes development of an mHealth ecological momentary intervention (EMI) for young adults with high-risk alcohol use and ADHD. The intervention uses EMA as an intervention component to increase self-awareness through symptom monitoring. It also includes personalized feedback and behavioral strategy suggestions (“tips”) tailored by EMA data. The article describes the theoretical basis, relevance for young adults with ADHD and high-risk alcohol use, use of EMA data to generate intervention content, participant feedback through iterative pilot testing, human-centered design with end users and community partners, and practical benefits/challenges/future opportunities.

## Curation note

Relevant to EMA/EMI, digital ADHD support, self-awareness, tailoring, and ADHD with high-risk alcohol use. It is a development/methods article, not evidence that the intervention improves outcomes.
