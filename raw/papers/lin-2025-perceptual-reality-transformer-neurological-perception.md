# Perceptual Reality Transformer: Neural Architectures for Simulating Neurological Perception Conditions

- Source: arXiv
- arXiv ID: 2508.09852v1
- Authors: Baihan Lin
- Published: 2025-08-13
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09852v1

## Abstract

Neurological conditions affecting visual perception create profound experiential divides between affected individuals and their caregivers, families, and medical professionals. The paper presents the Perceptual Reality Transformer, a framework employing six neural architectures to simulate eight neurological perception conditions with scientifically grounded visual transformations. The system learns mappings from natural images to condition-specific perceptual states, enabling approximations of simultanagnosia, prosopagnosia, ADHD attention deficits, visual agnosia, depression-related changes, anxiety tunnel vision, and Alzheimer's memory effects.

Through systematic evaluation across ImageNet and CIFAR-10 datasets, the paper reports that Vision Transformer architectures achieved optimal performance compared with traditional CNN and generative approaches. The work frames the system as a benchmark for neurological perception simulation, with possible applications in medical education, empathy training, and assistive technology development.

## Curation note

This is a broad perception-simulation paper, not an ADHD support or clinical efficacy study. The ADHD relevance is limited to the inclusion of simulated ADHD attention deficits and possible empathy/training applications.
