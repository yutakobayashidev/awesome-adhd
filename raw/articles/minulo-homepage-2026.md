## Extracted page content

minulo is described as an iPhone app that hides the short-video feeds inside social apps: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok For You and Snapchat Spotlight. Each network has its own switches, so the user can turn off Reels, Shorts or For You one by one. No minulo account is needed; the user signs in on each network's own login page.

The page says minulo uses Apple's Screen Time to lock the official apps. Tapping the usual Instagram icon out of habit opens Instagram inside minulo instead, with Reels already gone. Locking the official apps is free for up to 2 hours per block.

When a friend sends a Reel, the user can watch that one Reel, and no feed starts after it ends. Every week the app shows the time not spent on Reels and Shorts, and a streak counts the days in a row the lock stayed on.

Pro features listed on the page are Sleep mode (social apps lock at the same bedtime and unlock at the same wake time every day), Work mode (chosen apps lock during chosen workdays and hours), Hard Mode, a daily limit, and blocks longer than 2 hours.

The page lists exam study and going to sleep on time as typical uses, and says 84% of people who shared their age are 24 or younger (October 2026 data).
