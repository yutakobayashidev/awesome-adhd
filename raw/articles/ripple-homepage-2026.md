## Extracted page content

Ripple is described as a free, local-only app blocker for Android and iOS, built for moments when willpower drops. Tightening rules takes effect immediately. Adding time, skipping a focus schedule, pausing protection, or uninstalling requires waiting through a countdown (15 minutes by default, up to 2 hours) or entering a friend or parent unlock code.

The page lists an in-app feed blocker on Android that backs out of YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, WeChat Channels and Moments while keeping chats, DMs and long-form video usable, a 23:30 to 07:00 sleep schedule, and per-session whitelist caps.

Cooldown timers are said to run on time since boot, so changing the phone date, time or timezone does not skip the countdown. On iOS the page says it uses FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity from Apple's Screen Time API.

Phone calls, SMS, alarms, calendar, camera and keyboards are listed as always allowed.
